Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common inquiries regarding admissions, scholarships, transport, and campus safety.

When does the admission cycle for 2026-2027 open and close?

Early Decision applications open on September 1 and close on December 15. Regular decision applications are accepted on a rolling basis subject to seat availability in each grade division. We strongly recommend early submission as several divisions reach full capacity ahead of schedule.

What are the criteria for Merit and Need-Based Scholarships?

Beaconfield awards merit scholarships covering 15% to 50% of annual tuition to candidates demonstrating exceptional scholastic achievement, leadership aptitude, or artistic and athletic mastery. Applications require prior school transcripts and an entrance assessment.

Is school transportation available across the city?

Yes. We operate a fleet of GPS-equipped, air-conditioned buses covering 24 distinct routes across the metropolitan region. Each bus is staffed with a trained security chaperone, first aid equipment, and real-time parent tracking through our mobile portal.

What international accreditation does Beaconfield hold?

Beaconfield is an authorized IB World School (offering PYP, MYP, and DP), a registered Cambridge International School, and accredited by the Council of International Schools (CIS) and Cognia, guaranteeing seamless credit transfer worldwide.
